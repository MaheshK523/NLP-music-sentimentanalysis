# Example data rights

The four short lyric excerpts in `sample_songs.csv` and `sample_songs.jsonl`
were created specifically as synthetic fixtures for this repository. They do
not quote or adapt a released song and are distributed under the repository's
MIT license.

The CSV and JSONL files contain the same records so users can exercise both
input paths. They are demonstrations, not a labeled evaluation dataset, and
must not be used to claim model accuracy.
